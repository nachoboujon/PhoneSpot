    FAQ's

    The Infinix Smart 20 is powered by the MediaTek Helio G81 Ultimate processor. This efficient octa-core chipset ensures smooth day-to-day performance, quick app launches, and reliable multitasking for budget-conscious smartphone users.
    Yes, the Smart 20 is highly versatile when it comes to memory. Alongside its internal storage, it features a dedicated MicroSD slot that supports massive storage expansion of up to 2TB for all your media.
    The smartphone features a large 6.78-inch HD+ IPS display. It boasts an incredibly smooth 120Hz refresh rate, ensuring fluid scrolling, responsive touch interactions, and an immersive viewing experience for videos and social media.
    It comes equipped with a large 5200mAh battery designed to easily last through a full day of heavy usage. It supports 15W charging, and a 10W charger is conveniently included right in the box.
    The Dynamic Bar is a smart interactive software feature integrated into the Smart 20's display. It seamlessly surfaces important notifications, charging status, and background tasks around the front camera cutout for quick, easy access.
    The Infinix Smart 20 features an ultra-slim profile, measuring just 7.7mm in thickness. This sleek design makes the device highly ergonomic, lightweight, and very comfortable to hold or slide into your pocket.
    Yes, despite being an affordable device, the Smart 20 comes with an official IP64 rating. This means it is effectively protected against everyday dust ingress and accidental water splashes from any direction.
    The phone includes an 8MP rear camera with an f/2.0 aperture and autofocus, supported by a rear dual flash. It also features an 8MP front-facing camera, perfect for clear selfies and video calls.
    Yes, the Infinix Smart 20 is equipped with a fast and reliable fingerprint sensor. This provides an extra layer of biometric security, allowing you to unlock your smartphone quickly and securely with a single touch.
    The device runs on the latest XOS 16 interface, which is built on top of Android 16. This provides a clean, user-friendly software experience packed with intuitive features and modern privacy controls.
