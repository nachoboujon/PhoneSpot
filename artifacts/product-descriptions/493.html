FAQs
FAQs
1. Can Mibro GS Active2 sync with Android and iPhone?

Mibro GS Active2 is compatible with Android and iPhone. Download the Mibro Fit app and follow the on-screen steps to connect and sync your data.
2. Is the Mibro GS Active2 good for beginner runners?

Yes, Mibro GS Active2 is a good running smartwatch for beginners. It offers various running modes and custom plans, and can also track training and health data when you run.
3. Can Mibro GS Active2 track location, take calls, and show notifications?

Yes. GS Active2 running smartwatch has built-in dual-frequency GNSS for location. It can answer and hang up calls and also supports message notifications, which need to be enabled in the Mibro Fit app under “Me” → “Set up.”
4. Is Mibro GS Active 2 waterproof?

Mibro GS Active2 features 5 ATM water resistance, making it suitable for swimming, surfing, and snorkeling.

Water Resistance & Usage Tips

Limit each water contact to no more than 30 minutes. Avoid prolonged soaking.
Do not wear in hot showers or high-temperature, high-humidity, or steam-heavy environments. Steam may enter the watch and damage internal circuits.
Seawater use: Rinse promptly with fresh water after contact and use the built-in drainage function to prevent corrosion and maintain water resistance.
5. How bright is Mibro GS Active2 display?

GS Active2 running smartwatch features an AMOLED display with up to 1200 nits brightness.
6. How long does its battery last?

This running smartwatch offers up to 20 days of battery life in daily use and up to 15 hours in GPS mode.
7. Does it sync with Apple Health / Strava / Google Fit?

Yes. Simply complete the required connection and setup.
8. Does it support music apps like Spotify and Pandora?

GS Active2 running smartwatch can control Spotify and Pandora, but the apps must be opened on your phone and running in the background.
9. Are the built-in apps on a smartwatch free to use?

Yes. The built-in apps on the smartwatch, as well as the Mibro Fit app on your phone, are free to use.
10. What are the warranty and return policies?

Mibro GS Active2 comes with a 12-month warranty. Refunds are processed within 14 days after approval. See the Return & Refund page for details.
