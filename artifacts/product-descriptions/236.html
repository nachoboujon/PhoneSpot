Technical Specifications

  • Display: 6.9″ display, 720 × 1560 resolution, 120Hz refresh rate
  • Processor: Unisoc T615
  • Operating System: Android 16
  • Memory & Storage: 4GB RAM
  • Storage: 128GB storage ROM  
  • Rear Camera: 64MP main camera
  • Front Camera: 8MP front camera
  • Battery: 5,150mAh battery, 18W fast charging
  • Connectivity: 4G full bands, Wi-Fi, GPS, NFC
  • Network Type: 4G
  • Sensors & Features: Fingerprint sensor, NFC
  • Dimensions: 163.8 × 78.35 × 8.8 mm
  • Weight: 205g
  • Certifications: CE, ICASA, ISO9001, GMS
  • Colours: Orange, Blue, Silver
  • Warranty: 12-Month Limited Warranty with Local South African Support

FAQs

Is the Oukitel C17 Plus a rugged phone?
No. The C17 Plus is best positioned as a large-screen everyday smartphone rather than a rugged phone, as rugged IP and MIL-STD ratings have not been supplied for this model.

What storage options are available on the Oukitel C17 Plus?
The C17 Plus is available in 4GB RAM with 128GB storage and 6GB RAM with 256GB storage configurations. Oukitel South Africa currently has the 4GB + 128GB storage configuration.

Who is the Oukitel C17 Plus best suited for in South Africa?
The C17 Plus is suited to everyday users, staff phones, students and mobile workers who want a large smooth display, practical storage, NFC and reliable 4G connectivity.
