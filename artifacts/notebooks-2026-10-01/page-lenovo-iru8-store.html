    New Horizons of Mobility

    The Ideapad Slim 3 15IRU8 Arctic Grey (82X700GHRA) is a laptop that exceeds all expectations. With its incredibly lightweight design and slim body, it allows you to move freely and work anywhere. Weighing just 1.62 kg, you can carry it with you all day without feeling any discomfort.

    Expanded Screen Space

    Enjoy limitless possibilities with the IdeaPad Slim 3 15IRU8. Its 15.6-inch display delivers vibrant, clear, and realistic visuals. With a 16:9 aspect ratio and narrow bezels, you gain more screen space and improved productivity. Certified with TÜV Low Blue Light, the display reduces the impact of harmful blue light on your eyes, ensuring comfortable use throughout the day. Additionally, immerse yourself in high-quality audio with Dolby Audio technology, which delivers deep and realistic sound.

    Power and Performance

    The Ideapad Slim 3 is powered by the advanced 6-core Intel Core i3-1315U processor, providing the power and performance needed for various tasks. Designed for multitasking, it lets you easily run multiple applications simultaneously without delays. Its expanded storage allows you to keep all your files, photos, music, and videos. With a 512 GB SSD, you'll enjoy fast access to your data while being confident about its security.

    Maximum Comfort of Use

    The IdeaPad Slim 3 is designed for convenience on all levels. Its full-featured USB-C ports allow you to charge the device, transfer video signals, and quickly transmit data. Additionally, it features two USB 3.2 ports, one HDMI port, and a combo jack for connecting headphones. 

    The built-in webcam with a privacy shutter ensures a high level of privacy. Plus, with fast-charging support, just 15 minutes of charging provides up to two hours of laptop usage.

    Smart Features for Productivity

    The IdeaPad Slim 3 is equipped with intelligent features that enhance productivity. With Smart Power technology, the laptop adapts to your needs to maximize work efficiency. Smart Wireless technology provides a stable connection, allowing you to work seamlessly online. Built-in security features such as Natural File Guard ensure the safety of your data. Noise-canceling technology helps eliminate unnecessary background noise, ensuring a clear and focused experience.
