Features

Revolutionize Computing With Lenovo AI

This isn’t just another PC — it’s a game-changer. Fueled by Intel® Core™ Ultra H Series processors and a 13 trillion-operations-per-second (TOPS) neural processing unit (NPU), the IdeaPad Pro 5i accelerates compute-intensive applications and AI-driven tasks, tunes into your needs, knows when you're around, and keeps your data safe.

Creativity Unbound

Awaken your creative powers with a performance titan, featuring a robust 75W thermal design power for integrated graphics and 125W for discrete options. Pro-level graphics elevate every project, while Intel® Core™ processors and a 13 TOPS NPU deliver mind-bending speed. Optional NVIDIA® GeForce RTX® is perfect for demanding tasks and gaming.

Unleash Brilliance on a 16(40.64 cms)  OLED Display

Explore a 16(40.64 cms) 2.8K OLED display featuring a 16:10 ratio, and a 30-120Hz variable refresh rate. Plus, it’s easy on the eyes with TÜV certification, so you can binge-watch, game, or create without the strain. Watch your ideas pop off the screen in vivid detail, whether for study or fun.

Next-Level I/O: Connect, Transfer, Excel

Unlock next-level speed and connectivity with an enhanced I/O interface, tailored for the dynamic demands of the inspired. Includes USB-A and Thunderbolt™ ports for fast transfers, plus HDMI Fixed Rate Link (FRL) support for stunning visuals. Bring your ideas to life.

Get More Done With Intuitive Design

Elevate your experience with the IdeaPad Pro 5i, designed for seamless usability. Type on a crisp keyboard featuring 1.5mm key travel, a numeric keypad, and intuitive full-size direction keys. Power through with a robust 84wh battery, a large glass touchpad, and a flexible hinge for just the right angles. Smooth, smart computing. 

Credibility & Collaboration

Our goal is to provide smarter technology that builds a brighter, more sustainable future for our customers, communities, and the planet. That's why we pursue industry leading labels and certifications that demonstrate our commitment to sustainability in product design. Together, we can build a smarter future for all.
