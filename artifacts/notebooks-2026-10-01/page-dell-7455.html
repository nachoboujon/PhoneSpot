      What customers are saying
      Customers praise the Dell Latitude 7455 for its fast Snapdragon processor, quiet operation, and battery life that often exceeds 15 hours on a single charge. Some customers report that the ARM architecture prevents key business applications and drivers from installing, leading to workarounds or reduced functionality. Overall, most reviews highlight speed and endurance—such as a user noting 20‑hour usage—while a smaller group flags software incompatibility problems.
